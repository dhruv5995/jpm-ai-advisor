# Model Portfolios by Risk Band

Illustrative allocations used as a starting point for advisor recommendations.
Risk aversion is scored 1 (very conservative) to 5 (very aggressive), matching
the scale used in client profiles.

## Risk Band 1 — Capital Preservation
- 15% US large-cap equities
- 10% international developed equities
- 60% US investment-grade bonds
- 15% cash & equivalents
- Expected volatility: low. Priority: minimize drawdown risk, near-term
  liquidity.

## Risk Band 2 — Conservative Growth
- 30% US large-cap equities
- 15% international developed equities
- 45% US investment-grade bonds
- 10% cash & equivalents

## Risk Band 3 — Balanced
- 40% US large-cap equities
- 15% international developed equities
- 5% emerging market equities
- 35% US investment-grade bonds
- 5% cash & equivalents

## Risk Band 4 — Growth
- 50% US large-cap equities
- 20% international developed equities
- 10% emerging market equities
- 15% US investment-grade bonds
- 5% REITs

## Risk Band 5 — Aggressive Growth
- 55% US large-cap equities
- 20% international developed equities
- 15% emerging market equities
- 5% REITs
- 5% US investment-grade bonds

## Notes for advisors
- Clients within 5 years of a major goal (retirement, home purchase, tuition)
  should generally be shifted one risk band more conservative than their
  stated risk aversion alone would imply, regardless of age.
- Concentrated single-stock positions (>10% of investable assets in one
  name) should be flagged as a diversification risk independent of the
  model portfolio recommendation.
