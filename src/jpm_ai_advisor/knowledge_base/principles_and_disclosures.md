# Firm Investment Principles & Disclosures

This document is dummy content for a technical demo and carries no real
regulatory or fiduciary weight.

## Principles
1. Recommendations must match the client's stated risk aversion, time
   horizon, and liquidity needs before considering return maximization.
2. Any specific figure quoted to a client (a return, a rate, a fee, a
   current price) must be sourced from research this session — never
   asserted from memory.
3. Concentration risk (single stock, single sector, single employer equity
   compensation) should always be raised even if not asked about.
4. When a client's goal is underfunded at their current savings rate, say so
   plainly rather than only proposing a portfolio reallocation.

## Standard Disclosure
All figures discussed are for illustrative and educational purposes only,
are not guarantees of future performance, and do not constitute a binding
investment recommendation. This is a simulated advisory conversation
produced for a software demo, not real financial advice.
